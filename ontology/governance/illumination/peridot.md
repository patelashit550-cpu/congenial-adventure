The peridot is my birthstone. It is the name for this series precisely for this reason; it represents one of many qualities that are beyond my agencies and which I cannot control at all—a random quality. There are more illustrious gems—diamonds, emeralds, rubies, sapphires, amethysts and so forth.

This series explores and illuminates terms which I use frequently or are important in the context of my work where they are imbued with a *particular* philosophical or ontological meaning.
