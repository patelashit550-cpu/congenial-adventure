A poetic or romantic (mythical) representation of node. The word currant is derived from Corinth - an historical node for traffic between rivals Sparta and Athens. The word currency is too derived from it as that which had currency readily flowed like wine (made from currants) back and forth through Corinth. What passes is created by us in time - it is made with time (hours) in flow and is our collective intellectual property of human culture.
