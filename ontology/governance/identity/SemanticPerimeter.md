A coded boundary of shared meaning (*semper idem*) within which we recognize one another and can evaluate exchange with those beyond it. Nested perimeters compose the body politic.

Intelligence, Identity, and Capital are the living pillars inside that perimeter — the Dial Square is the modeling frame by which they cohere.
