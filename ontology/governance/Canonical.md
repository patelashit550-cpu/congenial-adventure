## Alpha

The force that builds utility through established harvest of the commons as an orthodoxy of established, competence, and the will to make things work inside inherited systems. In the S-curve of Areté it is the starting plateau; in the Trials of Job it contends extends through praxis to reach for Omega where truth is in balance with ecology through utility.

## Anarchism

Order without permanent coercive authority. A natural governance achieved through identity, community and alignment to humanity through the custody of ecology.

## Anima

Bare liveness — the uncostumed self beneath prestige, pride, and place. The peaceable aspect that discovers what is minimal and essential, and that refuses to strip-mine the commons for ego's display.

## Animus

Exuberant competition and defensive force — the public edge of the coin whose other face is anima. Necessary when a perimeter must be held; corrosive when it becomes shrill, paranoid, or growth for growth's sake (*machina* without *Dei*).

## Areté

Virtue: what it means to say that a thing is good. Soft power — the divine within ordinary people — as against Giants' hard power of money and office. Linguistically kin to French *arrêt*: the cultivated capacity to stop, desist, and arrest oneself as much as to commence.

## Autonomy

The capacity to live well free of arbitrary coercive authority and of the machina that steers you against your self-interest. Funded, practically, by surplus beyond food, clothing, and shelter — and undermined when money itself is debased.

## Call to Adventure

The Campbellian mandate to follow bliss despite turbulence — the summons one must answer even when the path is uncomfortable. Completes when Calling is known and named.

## Calling

The configuration of qualities and attractions through which meaningful work serves both self-actualization and the commons. Evidenced by sustained flow and useful outcomes rather than title or wage; the name one grows into when the adventure comes full circle.

## Capital

The ability and wherewithal to care for and sustain things — Utility in the Dial Square. Not merely money: the gear, know-how, and stewardship that make yield possible without hollowing the commons.

## Commons

The shared inheritance — climate, environment, institutions, and planetary trust — that no private perimeter may treat as free inventory. Custodianship of the commons is the ethical floor of governance and the red line of this corpus.

## Creation

The natural order of life itself — the eminence toward which loyalty finally points when pyramids and parties fail. To belong to the planet rather than a petrified hierarchy is to keep faith with Creation.

## D.E.C.A.Y

Dutifully Executing Conventional Activities Yieldlessly — the modern forfeiture of autonomy and Areté when ordinary people settle for a place in the pyramid instead of living as agents.

## Dial Square

The four-pillar model of a self-sovereign character binds Intelligence through Identity (_Truth_), Capital through Current (_Utility_), The Yoga Element through Good Form (_Alignment_), Connectedness and Integration (_Beauty_). Together, these core pillars converge to emanate a fourth - Governance, continuously regulated and unified by a self-sustaining Cyclicality ($\pi$).

## Firmitas

Architectural firmness — visual and structural harmony aligned with nature; the broadest consensus on an objective aesthetic (beauty). What praxis yields continuously as an evolution of a main branch orthodoxy (alpha) when edge exploration is done well: resilient form, not brittlle.

## Giants

Celebrity and institutional personalities who dominate the social network through capital and narrative — hard coercive power paid for by money to generate money. Their commitments often exceed what delegated execution can honour; credibility erodes when scale makes care impersonal.

## Governance

The perimeter of scope that combines Intelligence, Capital, and Identity without externalizing cost — and that may amend neighbouring perimeters for the better. Begins with governing oneself; radiates outward as positive agency.

## Hormesis

The discipline of drawing comfort from discomfort — virtue forged in turbulent flow. Praxis registers reality as data and grows Areté by surviving the stress that orthodoxy alone cannot metabolize.

## Identity

An ever-evolving understanding of one's place in the world — Beauty or Firmness in the Dial Square. The whole human, not a fragment distributed across platforms; the Stone Rose cultivated as enduring work on oneself.

## Intelligence

The ability to apply reason and empathy to discover a starting point: Truth. First pillar of the Dial Square; without it Capital and Identity become mere force and costume.

## Keiretsu

Stakeholder clusters around trading houses — human-centric anarchy founded on shared history rather than purely legalistic capitalism. An Origins lens on how networks can cohere without a permanent warden.

## Ki

Vital energy — 気 — and its structural kin (機 mechanism, 規 standard): the flow that must move through layers until it finds a legitimate base. Praxis keeps architecture from brittleness so that *ki* can reach wherever it is required.

## Ledger

The record of who owes what to whom — from Domesday to chain. Money as information; identity and value custody expressed through protocol-bound agreement signed by keys.

## Machina Animus

The control apparatus that induces behaviour contrary to self-interest — Giants' machinery of animus. Autonomy requires seeing it clearly enough to refuse its steering.

## Maya

The cosmic veil that conceals the true nature of reality — dense enough, in late systems, to exert coercive pull. To govern oneself is to break the spell of maya without denying the friction of the transition.

## Moral Injury

The wound from participating in, witnessing, or carrying out what violates core moral belief under institutions one was taught to trust. Distinct from ordinary burnout; central to Act II / Trials of Job.

## Namaste

The protocol that initiates interface with the divine in another — soft power without priest or king. The greeting that models how Giants' mediation becomes unnecessary when virtue recognizes virtue.

## Noblesse Oblige

The obligation of those with leisure and privilege to contemplate and act on behalf of the commons — assumed without demanding extra station in return.

## Omega

The attractor of wholesome, internalized, integrative functionality — destiny as sustainable and holistic rather than merely extractive. In the Trials of Job it pulls against Alpha; in the S-curve of Areté it is the far shore beyond Praxis.

## Orthodoxy

Might-makes-right as inherited form — Alpha's rigidity. Necessary as a base rate; fatal when it forbids Praxis. Thor to Loki's play: the dance that keeps a culture from freezing.

## Peridot

The authorial lens and site aesthetic — birthstone as colour of Illumination. The particular way this corpus looks at governance, identity, and the commons.

## Perimeter

A boundary of belonging, risk, and code — nested and overlapping — within which we align. Personal, communal, institutional, or planetary; always both constant and changing. The unit of Governance's scope.

## Political Economy

The practical lens on how institutions value and price things — learned at the shop counter as much as in theory. The ground from which this corpus reads capital, labour, and the Sovereign.

## Praxis

Fluid practice that loosens orthodoxy just enough to explore the edge cases of the social network. It yields resilient architecture (*firmitas*) aligned with nature, lets *ki* flow, and turns Alpha toward Beta on the path to Omega. Mobility without loss of discipline.

## Project, Product, Protocol

The compressed arc of change on the social network: from managed project, to owned product, to use-case-specific public protocols (Bitcoin, Ethereum, Solana as exemplars). Decentralizing agency and how value is registered.

## Satyagraha

Asymmetric truth-force — explanatory challenge that embraces the discomfort a regime offers because that friction nourishes the soul. Soft power against hard coercion in a liberal setting.

## Self-sovereign

Self-possessed, self-aware, self-sufficient, self-contained — then projecting only what is necessary onto the network. Inner sovereignty as the practical means of crossing thresholds without surrendering the whole self to harvest.

## Semantic Perimeter

A semantic perimeter of identity embodied (*semper idem*): the coded boundary in which we recognize shared meaning and can evaluate transactions with the *other* beyond it. Nested perimeters compose the body politic.

## Semper Idem

Identity embodied as a mound of attested interaction — stable yet forever evolving. The tell of who you are across contexts; the basis for trust that does not require loyalty theatre.

## Social Credit

Reciprocal trust registered by a community — how much others may safely entrust you with what matters inside a semantic perimeter. Not authoritarian scoring: preference and reliability made legible through protocol.

## Social Network

The social and intellectual ferment where identities transact — the fabric in which project becomes product becomes protocol. Architecture, *ki*, and Calling all play out here.

## Soft Power

Virtue as influence without coercion — Areté, namaste, the holy spirit as culture's quiet force. Contrasted with Giants' hard power of capital and office.

## Stone Rose

The cultivation of identity, virtue, and capability as the only enduring work worth building. Self as craft against a civilization that has inverted what is priceless and what is cheap.

## Tel

A stratified mound of civilisation — compressed layers of settlement that look like a plain hill. Also the intimate tel each person builds transaction by transaction; ledger and self as sediment.

## Tenable

Money and interaction fit for good-faith increments at civilizational scale — time as money that can finally be trusted. Pay your fare with tenable money, or tread softly.

## Tev

Gujarati તેવ — habit. Homophone and cousin to *tel*: personality as a mound of repeated action. We are each a mound of habits.

## The Young Idea

Beauty or structural integrity not yet milled from the grain of truth available to us — the idea before fear turns it aside. Origins and the unfinished promise of what could be built.

## Transition Insight

The framework for identity management and secure governance this corpus develops — the named project of bringing Dial Square, Calling, and the commons into one node.

## Truth, Utility, Beauty

The triad all of us believe in — satyam transmuted through shivam into sundaram. Anchors the pavilion of virtue and the Yoga Element's path from truth to beauty via purpose.

## Yield

Durable utility after the costs of time, energy, and governance — improvement that increases worthwhile capability or contentment, not merely a monetary return. Civilization's yield curve is a moral instrument before it is a market one.

## Yoga Element

Consciousness as three Vedantic aspects — primal, deep, and rational — through which beauty connects to truth by way of mission and utility. The missing link in Dial Square's living calculus.
